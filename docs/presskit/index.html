Short description
A fast co-op roguelite FPS set in 1887. Dive in a brass diving suit, fight through drowned ruins room by room and build absurd combos from weapons, charms and elemental blessings, until something at the bottom wakes up.

Long description

The Pacific, 1887. A research expedition finds the ruins of the Thalassari, a civilization that sank into the deep long ago. You strap into a brass diving suit and go down. Every dive leads through new rooms of temples, a drilling station, a coral cathedral, sunken archives and the abyss below them.

Each room is a fight. You pick your rewards between rooms: weapons with their own alternate fire, charms that stack, and elemental blessings that react with each other. Freeze and shatter, chain lightning through a horde, or open a black hole and watch everything get pulled in. Runs end with a boss, and each boss has a signature moment, such as Xal-Tuun pulling you into another world.

Between dives you return to your submarine to spend what you found and choose how deep to go next.

Key features

  • Fast movement with dash and double jump, built for arena fights
  • 5 areas, 5 bosses, 9 weapons with alternate fire, charms, blessings and element reactions
  • Online co-op for up to 4 divers
  • Steampunk 1887 meets Lovecraftian deep sea, with ruins that glow in the dark
  • Online leaderboards for the fastest runs, the highest pressure level and the most damage
